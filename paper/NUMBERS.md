# Where every number in the paper comes from

All paths are relative to the repository root. `results/*.json` hold every evaluation episode and a `summary` block
written by `scripts/evaluate.py`. `docs/RESULTS.md` is generated from them by `scripts/make_results.py`.
`paper/derived_numbers.json` is generated by `paper/make_figures.py` from `results/*.json` only (no simulation, no
training). Wilson intervals are `summary.ci95` (shown in the paper as percent, rounded to integers in Table II).
Checkpoints (`ckpt/*/meta.json`) are not committed (gitignored, large); their values are reproduced in the
training table of `docs/RESULTS.md`.

Regenerate: `python scripts/make_results.py && python paper/make_figures.py`.
